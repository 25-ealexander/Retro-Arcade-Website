GAME INFORMATION

PAC-MAN (1980)

Originally released in Japan as "Puck Man," this masterpiece by Toru Iwatani changed the gaming industry forever by moving away from "space shooters" to appeal to a wider audience.

DEVELOPER: Namco (Midway in North America)

THE OBJECTIVE: Navigate the maze, eating all 244 dots while avoiding the four ghosts: Blinky (Red), Pinky (Pink), Inky (Cyan), and Clyde (Orange). Eating a Power Pellet turns the ghosts blue, allowing Pac-Man to eat them for bonus points.

LEGACY: Pac-Man is often cited as the most famous arcade game of all time. It spawned the first "gaming mascot" and led to the "Pac-Mania" craze of the early 80s, including a hit song and a Saturday morning cartoon.

THE KILL SCREEN: Due to an 8-bit integer overflow, the game becomes unplayable at Level 256. Half the screen turns into a jumbled mess of symbols, making a "Perfect Game" impossible to continue past this point.

EVOLUTION OF PAC-MAN

1990s

Transition to 3D with Pac-Mania and Pac-Man World.

2000s

Reinvented visuals with Championship Edition.

2010s

Introduction of Ghostly Adventures and the ultra-fast CE DX+.

PRESENT

Modern hits like Pac-Man 99 and Museum+.
