GAME INFORMATION

DONKEY KONG (1981)

Created by Shigeru Miyamoto, Donkey Kong was the game that saved Nintendo of America. It introduced the world to "Jumpman," who would later become the global icon known as Mario.

DEVELOPER: Nintendo R&D1

THE OBJECTIVE: As Jumpman, you must climb complex construction scaffolds to rescue Pauline from a giant ape. You must jump over barrels, dodge fireballs, and destroy obstacles with a hammer to reach the top.

LEGACY: This was one of the first "platformers" ever created. It established the formula for narrative in video games, using cutscenes to show the giant ape climbing higher with the captive girl.

THE KILL SCREEN: Similar to Pac-Man, Donkey Kong has a fatal bug. On Level 22, the internal timer is calculated incorrectly, giving the player only a few seconds to live, making it impossible to complete the level.

EVOLUTION OF DONKEY KONG

1990s

The Donkey Kong Country trilogy on SNES revolutionized gaming with pre-rendered 3D graphics.

2000s

Experimentation with Donkey Konga (rhythm) and DK: Jungle Beat using bongo controllers.

2010s

A return to tough 2D roots with Donkey Kong Country Returns and Tropical Freeze.

PRESENT

The Mario vs. Donkey Kong series continues, and DK appears as a major star in the Super Mario Movie.
