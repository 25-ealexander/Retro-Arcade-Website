GAME INFORMATION

STREET FIGHTER (1987)

Before the world-famous sequel, the original Street Fighter introduced Ryu and Ken to the world. It laid the foundation for every fighting game that followed.

DEVELOPER: Capcom

THE OBJECTIVE: Travel to five countries to defeat ten opponents in hand-to-hand combat. You must master the secret "Special Moves"—the Hadouken, Shoryuken, and Tatsumaki Senpukyaku—to defeat the final boss, Sagat.

LEGACY: While the first game was difficult to control, it pioneered the "six-button" layout and the concept of input-based special attacks. It turned the arcade into a competitive arena where players could truly test their skills against one another.

FACT: The original arcade cabinet featured two massive "pressure pads." The harder you punched the pad, the stronger the attack was on screen. These were eventually replaced by the standard six-button layout to prevent injuries and machine damage.

EVOLUTION OF STREET FIGHTER

1990s

Street Fighter II explodes in popularity, followed by the technical Alpha and III series.

2000s

The franchise returns after a long hiatus with Street Fighter IV, bringing the series into 2.5D graphics.

2010s

Street Fighter V focuses on the competitive pro-circuit and esports integration.

PRESENT

Street Fighter 6 introduces the "World Tour" mode and modern controls for a new era of fighters.
