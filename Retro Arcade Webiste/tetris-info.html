GAME INFORMATION

TETRIS (1989)

Designed by Alexey Pajitnov at the Academy of Science in Moscow, Tetris is a puzzle masterpiece that crossed the Iron Curtain to become a global phenomenon.

DEVELOPER: Alexey Pajitnov / ELORG

THE OBJECTIVE: Clear horizontal lines by manipulating falling geometric shapes called Tetrominoes. As you clear lines, the game speeds up, testing your reflexes and spatial logic.

LEGACY: Tetris is considered the most ported game in history. Its inclusion as a pack-in title for the Nintendo Game Boy made it a staple of mobile gaming and proved that puzzle games could be system-sellers.

THE KILL SCREEN: For decades, Level 29 was thought to be the "kill screen" due to the speed, but in 2023, a young player finally pushed the game to Level 157, causing the software to crash for the first time.

EVOLUTION OF TETRIS

1990s

The era of The Next Tetris and Tetris DX, adding colorful visuals and saving progress.

2000s

Standardization with Tetris DS and the Tetris Worlds series, defining modern rotation rules.

2010s

The hypnotic Tetris Effect brings VR and music-syncing to the legendary puzzle gameplay.

PRESENT

Battle Royale competitive play with Tetris 99 and the release of the Tetris biographical film.
